Cite the nuclear data

Every macroscopic cross section on this page is computed from the microscopic cross sections of an evaluated nuclear data library: years of measurement, nuclear modelling and expert judgement by the evaluators who made it. If you use these plots or their data, cite the libraries they come from. The download carries the same references for each row it holds.

What is plotted is the library processed for yamc: neutron data processed with NJOY and Doppler broadened to the temperature chosen, converted to the Arrow tables yamc reads, and summed into macroscopic cross sections in your browser by yamc compiled to WebAssembly. No evaluated value was intentionally changed.
